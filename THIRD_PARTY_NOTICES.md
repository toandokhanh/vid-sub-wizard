# Third-party notices and research provenance

Original contributions and third-party material must be distinguished when reusing this academic repository. Dependency packages retain their own licenses; package-manager license metadata does not by itself license every file in this repository. No new repository-wide license is granted by this maintenance pass.

The subtitle recognizer is a legacy implementation shared across the author’s video research repositories. Its complete original upstream provenance has not yet been reconstructed; preserve existing source notices and do not assume a blanket MIT license. DeepFilterNet, Noisereduce, FFmpeg, pretrained speech/TTS models and datasets each retain their own distribution terms. Research paper authors and dataset references should be credited as listed in the README.
